About Me

My name is Robert Joseph Carter. I enjoy video games and building online communities where people can connect through their shared interests.

I created Carter Gaming Hub as a concept for a Discord community focused on Madden NFL and NBA 2K. I want players to be able to find teammates, exchange strategies, share highlights, and compete in friendly games.

My Community Goals

Build Connections

Help gamers meet new people and find teammates.

Encourage Competition

Organize friendly matches, leagues, and tournaments.

Keep Gaming Welcoming

Encourage respect, teamwork, and positive communication.
